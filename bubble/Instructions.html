At the start of the experiment, the people in this room were randomly divided into groups by the code on their slip, and you stay in the same group for every game. Your group has {{ size_word }} traders. All the other traders in your group are real participants in this room.

In each game, the traders in your group are placed in a random line, and you will not know your place when you decide. An asset is offered to the first trader for {{ start }}. The asset is worth nothing and never pays anything. The only reason to buy it is the chance to resell it to a trader later in line.

The asset is then offered to each trader in turn:

  • If a trader buys, the asset is offered to the next trader at twice the price.
  • If a trader does not buy, the asset is offered to the next trader at the same price.

Every trader is offered the asset exactly once, so the only possible prices are {{ prices }}. A price of {{ start }} could mean you are anywhere in line.

Deciding and getting paid

Before learning your place in line, you choose Buy or Don't buy at each price you could be offered. Only your choice at the price you are offered counts.

You will first play one practice game, which is not paid. You will then play two parts of {{ C.GAMES_PER_PART }} games each, {{ n_games }} games in total. In every game, you get a new random place in line within your group. Right after each game, your screen shows your place in line, whether you bought the asset, and how much you earned in that game.

Only one of the paid games counts for payment. Every game has the same chance of being chosen, everyone in this room is paid for that same game, and you will find out which game it is at the end of the session. Since any game could be the one that counts, treat each one as if it determines your payment.

You get {{ fee }} for coming, plus between $0 and {{ max_earn }} from the game that counts, so you will be paid between {{ fee }} and {{ max_total }} in total.
